Introducing AquaTwin: a physics-informed, self-calibrating digital twin for seawater desalination that rehearses disturbances before they happen, screens every recommendation against hard limits, and withholds its advice when conditions leave what it has learned. Research prototype, all results simulated. Built by Team Kanban (https://kanbanstudios.ae/team-kanban) — try it at https://aquatwin.kanbanstudios.ae
