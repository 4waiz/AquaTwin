# AquaTwin: one-minute pitch (Khalifa University–UNESCO Global Water Hackathon 2026).
# One line per beat; each line becomes a cut. Numbers and acronyms are written as spoken.
In the Gulf, cities drink the sea.
Yet plants still run on fixed setpoints, and alarms that fire once the trouble has started.

Meet AquaTwin: a physics-informed AI digital twin that stays calibrated to the plant as it runs.
Physics predicts. Machine learning corrects the rest. And every answer carries its uncertainty.
Before a salinity shock, it rehearses the day ahead. Fixed setpoints break limits for twenty-one hours. AquaTwin's plan breaks none.
It flags membrane fouling half a day before cleaning is due.
Its safety layer, AquaGuard, checks every limit, and outside what the model knows, it withholds its advice.
Tested against a hidden plant, fully reproducible, and built for shadow mode on data plants already record.
Smart water, for S D G six.

AquaTwin. Predict, simulate, adapt. By Team Kanban.
