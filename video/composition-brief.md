# Hyperframes Composition Brief: AquaTwin

## Objective
A one-minute film of the working AquaTwin prototype for the KU–UNESCO Global Water Hackathon 2026 submission: what it does, shown in the real software, with a calm voice-over.

## Output
- Composition directory: `video/composition/` (generated by `video/tools/build_composition.py`)
- Rendered video: `video/brag.mp4`, poster `video/brag.jpg` (baked in as frame 0)
- Captions: burned in, and `video/captions.srt`
- Format: landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps
- Duration: 59.5 s (explicit one-minute brief; the 15–25 s /brag default does not apply)

## Source material
- Project root: this repository. Footage recorded from the production build (`npm run build`, served locally) by `video/tools/record_footage.py`, which also logs the time of every on-screen event (clicks, violation chip, withheld banner) in `assets/footage/footage.json`.
- Product name: AquaTwin
- Strongest claims, as shown in the app: "Stress-test the plant before the plant is stressed."; "LOW MODEL CONFIDENCE · RECOMMENDATION WITHHELD"; "All AquaGuard constraints held for 24 h".
- Key UI moments: Digital Twin equation (physics · 0D + ML residual = AquaTwin); Train 2 "Cleaning due · pressure drop +25 %"; Scenario Lab salinity shock (no action → CONSTRAINT VIOLATED; AquaTwin response holds every constraint); 676 candidate strategies and RECOMMENDATION APPROVED; out-of-distribution probe at a compound extreme; validation inside vs outside the envelope.
- Copy that must appear verbatim: "AquaTwin." / "Predict. Simulate. Adapt." / "Before the plant is forced to react." / "Built by Team Kanban · kanbanstudios.ae/team-kanban" / "Research prototype · all results simulated".

## Creative direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Direction: calm engineering film, the real software, no hype.
- Interpretation: few, readable holds; cuts keyed to real events; slow push-ins toward the UI detail that matters; captions as subtitles, not slogans; no exclamation marks.
- Angle: physics first, machine learning only where the physics is wrong, hard limits that are never traded off, and the honesty to say "I don't know". Every number on screen is simulated, and the film says so.
- Hook: the dark, dry 3D plant behind two lines ("Gulf desalination plants run on fixed setpoints and fixed alarms." / "By the time an alarm fires, the plant is already reacting."), then water pours and the twin comes alive (beat-locked to the 8.74 s cue).
- Outro: end card with the product line and credit.
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, abstract filler, any implication of endorsement by a named organisation.

## Visual identity (from the app)
- Background `#04060a`; text `#e8edf4`; accent `#5b9dff` (end-card tagline `#9cc3ff`).
- Fonts: Geist and Geist Mono (the app's fonts; Latin subsets bundled under `assets/fonts/`, SIL OFL 1.1).
- Footage treatment: none; UI pixels are left untouched (media-use "UI Fidelity"); attention is directed with framing and motion only.

## Storyboard (as built; times in seconds)
1. Problem · 0–8.7 · dark plant still, hook lines (the narration is on screen as type; no captions).
2. Living twin · 8.7–20.2 · the real intro: water pours, the plant activates; Digital Twin orbit; push-in to the equation ("…and corrects it with machine learning", 15.1) and to "Cleaning due" ("Train 2 … is due for cleaning", 18.5).
3. Rehearse the disturbance · 20.2–30.8 · Scenario Lab, salinity shock: No action → play → CONSTRAINT VIOLATED (26.0) → AquaTwin response (28.2): all constraints held.
4. Search and screen · 30.8–39.6 · 676 candidates, then the AquaGuard verdict panel.
5. Knowing when not to answer · 39.6–45.8 · Compound extreme (40.1) → LOW MODEL CONFIDENCE · RECOMMENDATION WITHHELD (43.2).
6. Honest evidence · 45.8–52.3 · Validation: prediction accuracy inside, then outside the envelope (48.8).
7. End card · 52.3–59.5.

## Audio
- Role: warm bed under narration, sparse accents.
- Narration: Kokoro `af_heart`, speed 1.05, seven lines (`assets/vo/`, script in `video/voiceover.md`).
- Music: "Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 12" (ende.app, bundled with /brag; not redistributed in this repository; see `assets/music/README.md`). Bed at 0.14 with a 1.2 s fade-in, a small lift to 0.22 after the last line, 1.6 s fade-out.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset (109.96 BPM). Beat-locked: pour 8.742 s (strong cue), end-card title 52.918 s; beat-grid: tagline 54.00 s, credit 56.75 s, withheld banner 43.16 s (beat 43.10).
- Audio-reactive treatment: not implemented; the hyperframes-creative extraction helper is not installed in this environment. The end-card glow breathes on a slow ease instead.
- SFX (Kenney, CC0), each on the visible event: soft impact at the pour, soft drop on the violation chip, click on the branch switch, deep bell on WITHHELD, bell on the end-card title.
